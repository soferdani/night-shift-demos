send-rail — the board night 5/5

For four nights this board opened on a message that was 33 days old, that somebody had asked for in writing, and that said tap the Gmail link, send. That instruction could not be carried out. The message announces three attachments, and no deep link on any platform carries a file.

Nights 1–5 of phase one all asked whether the text was finished — addressed, not stale, not argued with. All four detectors can pass on a message that is still impossible to send from a phone, because what is missing was never in the text: a file on a disk, a draft already sitting in a thread, or a condition the note set on sending. Night 5 asks that question instead, and splits the board on the answer. Sample data below.

Why this is the night-5 milestone

1

A new question: can his thumb finish it?

Not “is the text finished”. Three things say no, and all three are written down rather than guessed: the message announces attachments, a draft of it already exists somewhere a compose link cannot reach, or the note set a condition on sending.

2

The first card is now one he can actually send

On the real vault that moved the board off a 33-day-old reply that needs three PDFs and a desk, and onto a three-day-old one with nothing to attach — the one in the pile with a number in it.

3

Nothing is hidden or ranked down

The ones a phone cannot send get their own sections. Needs the laptop carries the checklist of what to attach, so the hunting is done before he sits down. Waits on something first quotes the line that said not yet.

4

A harmful tap is removed, not kept

Where a draft already exists, a compose link opens a new, empty, threadless message beside the finished one, with none of the attachments — and the typed text makes it look done. The tap now searches Gmail for the draft’s own name.
