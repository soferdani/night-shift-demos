Night Shift demos

Live pages built overnight by the Intern. Each runs on sample data in your browser, nothing to install.

  • The first number — the tool for the free hournight 1/5

    Five engineering offices are about to get a message that ends with one promise: sit with me for an hour and you walk out with a number — how many engineer-hours a month go on the paperwork, and what that is worth in shekels. Nothing produced that number. This does. Ask the six questions out loud, tap the answers, and the sheet he keeps builds itself: each leak with its own hours and rate, ranked, and a total he can add up by hand — because every row is rounded first, so the column always sums to the total printed under it. No figure comes from the market; there is no industry average anywhere in it, only his own answers. If the total is small the page says so and tells Dani to shake hands and go, which is what the pitch already promises out loud. Hebrew, right to left, prints to one A4 page, and no price appears on it.

  • Atidi Labs — the first page the name hasnight 1/5

    The business got its name on 22.11 and both domains were bought the same day. They point at nothing. An owner who reads the email and searches the name finds no page, so the name is still only a word in a note. This is the page the two domains can point at: Hebrew, phone-first, the four-step method with no prices on it, and one ask — the free hour at their office. No price appears anywhere, because the price note says the numbers are not confirmed and no owner has heard them yet. No client is named, and nobody is called a co-founder.

  • Agent Registershipped · 5/5

    Every AI agent running inside a company, on one sheet: what it may do, whose identity it acts as, whose data it can reach. Fill it while the CISO talks, print the register and the questions nobody could answer, in Hebrew or in English. Of the 23 questions, six have no Israeli legal hook at all — and those six are the ones that make an agent an agent. Night 5: press דוגמה: עוזר אחד for the shape the whole thing was built for — one assistant, twelve tools, the boss's own login. Until tonight that register answered all 23 questions and printed an empty questions sheet, because every check the tool had needed either a blank field or two agents sharing an account. So it now reads itself back a third way, by system: one assistant is twelve rows, and a system is the one place two agents meet without sharing a login.
