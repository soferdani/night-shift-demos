Night 3/5 · does the join exist, and where does the chain break?

Night 1 called leg A dead: a blind broker listing hides the business name on purpose, so there is nothing to match on. That is still true for almost every listing. It is not true for one kind.

A franchise resale cannot hide what it is — the brand is most of what's being sold, so it stays in the headline. And the SBA loan row carries that brand in a field of its own, sitting right beside the legal entity the listing is withholding. So tonight the join runs brand to brand instead of name to name.

Reaching a row is leg A solved. Whether the row answers is leg B. Collapsing those two into one number is exactly the mistake night 1 made.

No listings, no matching, no scraper. This is the share of franchise rows a brand + city key could ever point at unambiguously. It is the number to run against the real extract before anyone builds anything.

The brand list comes out of the loan file itself

No external brand list is used. The SBA Franchise Directory came back on 1 June 2025 and gives every brand an identifier code, which is the right source — and sba.gov has been unreachable from the build container for three nights. Bootstrapping from the file has a cost: a brand enters the list only once some row carries it, so a resale of a brand the extract never mentions is invisible here, not unmatched. One third-party analysis puts FranchiseName coverage at 8.7% of FY2010–2019 records. Unconfirmed at source.

Still true, and still the thing that matters

No price. Not the asking price, not what it sold for, not the multiple. This is a confirmed sale, never a comp — nothing public carries the number.

And nobody has yet been asked whether they'd pay for it. Three nights of building haven't touched that, and a fourth won't either.
